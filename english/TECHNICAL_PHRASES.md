# Technical Phrases

Choose one or two phrases for today's answer. Complete them with a specific
reason, observation, or example. Use ordinary words and your own phrasing;
this is a small reference, not a list to memorize.

## Starting an explanation

- At a high level, ...
- This is useful when ...
- Internally, this works by ...
- For example, ...

## Decisions and tradeoffs

- The main reason we chose this approach was ...
- Another option we considered was ...
- One tradeoff is ...
- This improves ..., but it also ...

## Debugging

- After investigating the logs, ...
- We found that ...
- If that looks normal, I would next ...
- I would narrow the problem down by ...

## System design and performance

- I would first clarify the requirements ...
- From a scalability perspective, ...
- From a performance perspective, ...
- If traffic increased significantly, ...

## Project explanations

- My contribution was ...
- The bottleneck was ...
- We initially assumed ..., but the evidence showed ...
- To prevent this from happening again, ...

## DSA reasoning

- The straightforward approach would be ...
- We can avoid repeated work by ...
- The invariant is that ...
- The time complexity is ..., because ...; the extra space is ...

Use “we” for team decisions and “I” for your own contribution. Use “I would”
for hypothetical approaches rather than implying you implemented them.
For instance, replace “It was fast” with “This reduced the number of database
queries” when that is the actual reason.
