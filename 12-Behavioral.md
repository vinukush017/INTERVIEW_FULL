# Behavioral Interview Preparation

Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Spend most of the answer on your actions and the result.

- [ ] A difficult bug you solved
- [ ] A disagreement with a teammate
- [ ] A missed deadline or failure
- [ ] A time you showed ownership
- [ ] Ambiguous requirements
- [ ] Competing priorities
- [ ] Critical feedback you received
- [ ] A process you improved

Prepare one flexible story for each prompt and add concrete results.
